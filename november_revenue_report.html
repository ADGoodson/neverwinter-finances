November 2026 Revenue Report

What Must Happen to Keep the Kingdom Solvent

Report Date: October 30, 2026

🚨 The Situation

As of October 26, the kingdom has $2,930.97 in unencumbered (operational) funds. At the current monthly burn rate of ~$213, this provides only 13.8 months of operational runway.

However, the November event is committed to draw $6,200 in encumbered funds. If the event costs more than it generates in ticket sales, the kingdom will draw down unencumbered reserves to cover the shortfall.

November is the deciding month. If the event generates strong revenue, the kingdom survives to 2027. If it underperforms, December goes negative.

Unencumbered Cash $2,930.97

Encumbered for November Event $6,200.00

November Revenue Target

To stay solvent through December and avoid a crisis in January, November must generate at least $1,500 in total revenue (pre-registrations + gate sales).

Minimum Revenue Target $1,500.00
Estimated Event Costs $800.00
Expected Net from November +$700.00
Unencumbered Balance After November ~$3,600.00

This assumes the event generates $1,500 in revenue. Current pre-registrations are only $631.78. Gate sales must add $868+ to reach the target.

What If November Misses the Target?

Scenario 1: November Generates Only $800 (Current Pace)
If gate sales match pre-registration levels, the event generates no net. The kingdom spends $800 from unencumbered reserves, leaving only $2,130.97 going into December. This triggers the crisis: operations continue to burn $213/month, and by mid-January the kingdom hits $0 unencumbered cash.
Scenario 2: November Generates Only $631 (Worst Case)
If the event loses money (event costs exceed revenue), the kingdom must draw $6,200 from reserves to pay for the committed event. This immediately takes unencumbered balance to NEGATIVE $3,269. The kingdom is insolvent before the February event even happens.
Scenario 3: November Generates $2,000+ (Recovery)
If the event generates strong sales (3x current pre-registration rate), the kingdom builds back to $5,500+ unencumbered. This provides runway through February and into Q1 2027, buying time to recover event attendance.

Critical Action Items for November

  • Marketing Push: Advertise November event aggressively. Current pre-sales ($631.78) suggest weak awareness. Need gate sales 3-4x higher than pre-registrations.
  • Ticket Price Analysis: Are current ticket prices optimal? Lower prices drive volume; higher prices maximize per-ticket revenue. Test the math.
  • Gate Day Logistics: Plan to capture walk-in/last-minute registrations. Most revenue typically comes at the gate, not pre-sales.
  • Backup Plan: If November looks weak by mid-month, decide now whether to scale event costs down or cancel/reschedule February event to reduce risk.
  • Finance Communication: Track actual November revenue daily. Don't wait until November 30 to discover shortfall. Adjust operations in real-time if needed.

December & Beyond

If November hits $1,500+: December is safe. Continue planning the February event with confidence that there's unencumbered buffer.

If November misses $1,500: December becomes critical. Operational costs must be cut, February event must be scaled back, or external funding sought immediately.

The February event ($6,200 encumbered) depends entirely on November's outcome. If November leaves the kingdom at minimal unencumbered balance, February event may not be affordable.

Bottom Line

November must generate $1,500 minimum to keep the kingdom on a sustainable path through Q1 2027. This requires gate sales to reach 3-4x current pre-registration levels. The Board needs a marketing plan and real-time tracking to make this happen. Waiting until November 30 to assess performance is too late—course corrections must start now.
