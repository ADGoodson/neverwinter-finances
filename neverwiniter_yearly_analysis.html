Key Findings & Patterns

  • 2022-2023
    Foundation Building
    Early stage with minimal activity. Accumulated base of ~$10-12K with virtually no expenses.
  • 2024
    Growth Phase
    First major activity year. Revenue grew 5.6x ($1,144 → $6,977). Balance grew from $10.4K to $16.3K (+56%).
  • 2025
    Peak Performance
    Best year yet. Revenue peaked at $25,738. Despite high expenses ($20,245), balance grew to $21,837 (+34%). Operations scaled successfully.
  • 2026
    Crisis Year
    Revenue collapsed 82% ($25,738 → $4,470). Expenses remain at unsustainable levels ($8,082). Balance declining (-10.2%). This trajectory leads to insolvency.
  • 2022-2026 Trend
    Encumbrance Problem
    As major encumbered events were booked (Crown Crucible, Nov event, Feb event), they transformed the kingdom from growth to debt. Treasury went from accumulation to depletion mode.
  • Future Outlook
    Unsustainable Trajectory
    If 2026 trend continues (high expenses, low revenue), operational reserves will be exhausted by Q2 2027. Kingdom cannot sustain current encumbered event schedule without major operational changes.
