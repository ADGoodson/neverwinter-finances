Key Findings

Foundation Building (2022–2023)
The kingdom started with minimal resources ($9,191 in May 2022) and grew steadily through early events. No major expenses yet; purely accumulating operating capital.
Growth Phase (2024)
Strong event-driven revenue ($10,894) with controlled expenses ($5,058). First year with significant event operations. Efficiency ratio: 2.2:1 (strong).
Peak Performance (2025) ★
The kingdom's best year: $32,522 in deposits, well-attended events, growing community. Ended with $23,429 in reserves. Revenue:Expense ratio 1.2:1 (healthy surplus).
Crisis Phase (2026) 🚨
Revenue collapsed 83% from 2025. Only $5,792 deposits in 8 months (vs. $32,522 in 2025). Expenses exceed income. Running structural deficit. Ratio: 0.75:1 (unsustainable).
What Changed?
2025 sustained events with strong attendance and ticket sales. 2026 shows collapsed ticket sales ($631.78 in October = 23% of 2025 monthly average). Community engagement and event interest declined sharply.
The Runway Problem
At 2025 performance: 9+ months of operations. At 2026 performance: 6–8 months before operational crisis. If 2026 trend continues, kingdom faces insolvency by Q1 2027 without revenue recovery or cost cuts.

The Bottom Line

2025 proved the kingdom can succeed: Strong events generated $32k+ in revenue, covering expenses and growing reserves to $23k.

2026 proves the kingdom cannot sustain decline: Revenue dropped 83%; the kingdom is burning capital instead of building it. Without immediate action, operational funds hit zero by December 2026.

The decision is now: Can the Board recover 2025-level event attendance and ticket sales, or must it scale back event commitments to match realistic 2026 revenue? The November and February events will determine whether the kingdom survives to 2027.
